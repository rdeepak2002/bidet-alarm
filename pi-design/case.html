Raspberry Pi 4 case

For the bidet alarm. Two parts, no screws. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom. ← Back to the build plan

Base, print/pi4_case_base.stl

Print open side up, no supports. The Pi drops onto 4 pegs.

Lid, print/pi4_case_lid.stl

Print top face down, no supports. The 4 posts press the Pi onto the pegs and the lip keeps the lid in place.

Openings

  • USB-C power: 18 × 12.5 mm, fits a chunky plug
  • One USB-A opening for the speaker: the lower USB 2.0 port (black), 18 × 11 mm. Other USB ports and Ethernet are covered.
  • Lid slot over the whole 40-pin header (▲ marks pin 1)
  • The SD card stays inside, with room for it sticking out about 3 mm. Vents on top and bottom.

Size

96 × 64 × 26.5 mm closed. Micro-HDMI and the audio jack are covered: the Pi runs headless and the speaker is USB.
